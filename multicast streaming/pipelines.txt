To capture video from multicast streaming:
gst-launch-1.0 -v udpsrc multicast-group=239.255.0.1 auto-multicast=true port=5000 caps="application/x-rtp, media=video, encoding-name=H264, payload=96, clock-rate=90000" ! rtph264depay ! h264parse ! avdec_h264 ! videoconvert ! autovideosink sync=false

To capture video from ip streaming:
gst-launch-1.0 rtspsrc location="rtsp://admin:kluz5056@192.168.0.121:554/Streaming/Channels/101" latency=0 protocols=udp drop-on-latency=true ! rtph264depay ! avdec_h264 ! videoconvert ! autovideosink sync=false
